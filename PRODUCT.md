# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Carriers (Transportistas):** Independent freight operators who use the platform to accept shipment requests, manage routes, and get paid. Mobile-first context — checking status on the road or from a cab.

**Shippers (Expedidores):** Businesses or individuals who need freight moved. Desktop-leaning — creating shipments, tracking status, paying for deliveries. The counter-party to every carrier.

Both personas share a single product surface but experience role-specific views (carrier dashboard vs. shipper dashboard). The primary task on any screen is checking or advancing a shipment's status.

## Product Purpose

Truckr® is a transportation services marketplace that connects independent carriers with shippers for freight transport in Argentina. The product's job is to reduce friction on both sides: carriers get consistent work, shippers get reliable transport without managing a fleet. Success is a shipment that moves from offer → accepted → in transit → delivered with clear status at every step.

## Brand Personality

Profesional · Cálido · Moderno

The interface should feel like a trusted logistics partner, not a cold enterprise system. Warmth comes from the committed two-voice palette (sky blue for shipper, honey saffron for carrier); modernity from sharp typography and purposeful motion. Professionalism means hierarchy is always clear and status is never ambiguous.

## Anti-references

**Generic SaaS cream:** Bland white-and-gray dashboards with no personality (early Notion, Airtable clones). Truckr has a committed palette — color carries real surface coverage, not a timid 5% accent.

Additional implicit anti-references: consumer-app bubbly (Rappi/Pedidos Ya pastel gamification), corporate navy-gold (DHL/FedEx enterprise freight), dark-mode logistics density (Uber Freight).

## Design Principles

1. **Two voices, one system:** Carrier (honey saffron) and shipper (open-sky blue) have distinct visual identities within the same design language. Every surface should signal which persona is active.
2. **Status is never ambiguous:** Freight moves through defined states. The interface must make the current state and next required action immediately legible without reading.
3. **Warmth over sterility:** Cream surfaces, tinted neutrals, and the saffron warmth distinguish Truckr from generic SaaS gray. Neutrals always carry a color tint.
4. **Committed, not tentative:** Color coverage is real — 30–60% of surfaces carry brand hues. Not a single timid accent on white.
5. **Typography earns hierarchy:** Unbounded for display/labels, Alegreya Sans for reading. Scale + weight contrast carry hierarchy; never rely on color alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Semantic color is always paired with text, icon, or shape — never color alone. Focus states are visible and keyboard navigation is complete. Reduced-motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
